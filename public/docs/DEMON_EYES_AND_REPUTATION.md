## Demon Eye Fantasy

Demon eyes are powerful cursed artifacts that grant passives and active abilities, but visibly mark the player as dangerous in many societies.

Core VR mechanic:

> The player closes one eye to activate the demon eye, gaining power while partially losing vision.

## Social Consequences

| Group | Reaction |
|---|---|
| human civilians | fear, avoidance, refusal to speak |
| merchants | refusal, overcharge, or guards |
| guards | suspicion, following, arrest for minor offenses |
| priests | refusal to heal or hostile cleansing |
| bandits | respect or fear |
| demons | admiration, curiosity, or faction interest |
| dwarves | pragmatic suspicion |

## Concealment

| Method | Benefit | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| eyepatch | reliable concealment | reduced vision |
| illusion monocle | preserves sight | can fail under inspection |
| hood / mask | crowd concealment | suspicious in guarded areas |
| glamour charm | magical disguise | cost or limited duration |

## Accessibility

Eye penalties need comfort options:

| Mode | Effect |
|---|---|
| immersive | one eye fully occluded |
| comfort | dark vignette or blur |
| minimal | subtle overlay plus mechanical penalty |

## Suspicion System

| Level | Behavior |
|---|---|
| 0 | normal |
| 1 | nervous dialogue |
| 2 | refusal or watchfulness |
| 3 | guards follow, citizens flee |
| 4 | search, fine, demand explanation |
| 5 | arrest, attack, expulsion |
